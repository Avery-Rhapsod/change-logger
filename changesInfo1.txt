XML tag
dogChgLst               Main container holding the document's change history
docChg                  Represents a change made at document level
chgData                 Contains information about who made the change and when
docMkLst                Contains document-level creation markers
docMk                   Marks a document-related creation event
sldChg                  Represents a change associated with a specific slide
sldMkLst                Contains markers identifying the relevant slide
sldMk                   Identifies a slide involved in the change
spMkLst                 Contains markers identifying the affected shape
spMk                    Identifies a particular shape or object
graphicFrameChg         Represents a modification to a graphic frame, such as a table, chart or other embedded graphic object
graphicFrameMkLst       Contains markers identifying the affected graphic frame
graphicFrameMk          Identifies a particular graphic frame
